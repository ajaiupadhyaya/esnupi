03 · The tenor trade

Buy three years, sell by the month

The grid reproduces Liquid Compute's worked example: 64 B300 GPUs bought at a fixed cost and re-let monthly. Cells show annual gross in $K. The simulation below asks what the grid can't: how often the trade loses money once price and fill move together.

Cells at or below zero are shaded red. The contour between is breakeven fill: cost ÷ re-let price.
Model. 4,000 monthly price paths over 36 months: lognormal moves plus release-date jumps. Fill drops as price drops, so bad months hit twice. The hedge sells a cash-settled forward on part of the book against an index with basis noise.
Calibration. Start $5.30; mean month-12 price ≈ $5.11, above LC's quoted $4.85 forward. Selling forward at $4.85 pays that gap as a risk premium, so hedged means sit below unhedged ones.
